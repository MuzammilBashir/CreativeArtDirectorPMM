## Diagnosis
Primary failure: Art Director direction. The prompt explicitly requested quietly tired but not miserable, slightly raised shoulders, focused weary eyes, and a relaxed closed mouth. The story reinforces restraint with a tiny breath, faint smile, and no exaggerated transformation. This suppresses the opening contrast needed to sell exhaustion and subsequent relief. Outputs broadly follow that restrained emotional direction: downward gaze and mild weariness, without a strongly collapsed silhouette or heavy eyelids.
Character specification: adult, short wavy hair, and realistic proportions; no male identity or masculine design direction was specified. Soft facial design therefore cannot fairly be called a model violation. Proposed correction for this concept: explicitly a fictional adult man, broader angular jaw and brow, simplified facial planes, optional light stubble. These are design choices, not universal gender rules.
Secondary execution failures: A exceeds the requested simplified material detail, adds a plant, and crops peripheral props. Both reverse the prescribed hand assignment. B is substantially wider and changes the character. B was submitted independently with text referring to Option A but no image reference, so identity drift was also a pipeline error.
