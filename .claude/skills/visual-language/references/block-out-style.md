Studs have gently curved tops, a small upper-edge highlight and a darker lower contact shadow. The purple frame is continuous and subtly textured, while the block surfaces are smooth. Depth comes from highlights, darker sidewalls and tight contact shadows. Special obstacles and UI shown in store screenshots are not part of unrelated concepts by default.

## Apply to block games

- Prefer clean silhouettes and consistent modest extrusion with broad smooth faces. Flat faces does not mean flat lighting or no depth.
- Use softened edges and controlled soft gloss. Preserve a narrow upper-edge highlight and darker lower edge; avoid glass rims, metallic shine and inflated pillow-like bodies.
- Keep studs regularly spaced, with gently rounded tops and readable low sidewalls, not flattened discs. Preserve the active game's count and spacing; four studs per cell is specific to the current prototype, not all puzzles.
- Use clear saturated pink, red, orange, yellow, lime, green, cyan, blue and violet families as reference vocabulary; avoid a washed-out pastel or grey cast. The reference also uses warm off-white and charcoal. Do not add those pieces or copy its checkerboard layout. Preserve distinct block identities and update matching gates together when refining colors.
- Use coherent soft lighting, brighter upper/left bevels, darker lower/right sidewalls and tight soft contact shadows at stud bases and adjoining pieces. This lighting direction is a visual inference, not a measured light rig. Avoid heavy black outlines or removing all occlusion. Judge silhouette separation at phone size.
- Prefer a quiet continuous frame over glossy decorative segments; retain the level's exact gate spans.
- Keep the frame predominantly matte purple with optional very fine texture; blocks remain smooth plastic. Do not apply the frame texture to blocks. The bright white burst at the top of the reference is an exit effect, not the normal material highlight.
- Treat the reference as a starting finish/readability benchmark, not a requirement to duplicate its levels, branding, UI or special obstacles.

The supplied screenshots still contain studs: “simple blocks” does not by itself instruct removal of studs. For other puzzle genres, transfer hierarchy and restrained detail without imposing plastic blocks on unrelated subjects.
