## Shared prompt

Create one portrait 9:16 static opening frame for a 4.5-second animated hook titled A Moment That's Mine. A single original fictional adult in their early thirties sits at a compact home desk, facing a partly open unbranded slate-gray laptop. Short dark wavy hair, muted teal long-sleeve crewneck, realistic adult proportions with simplified game-art shading. The person looks quietly tired but not miserable: slightly raised shoulders, focused weary eyes, relaxed closed mouth. Their right palm rests on the upper edge of the laptop lid, ready to close it; the lid is still open enough to make the action clear. Left hand rests naturally on the desk. A plain dark phone lies face down beside the laptop on the person's left, reachable with that free hand. A warm muted coral mug and one small tidy paper stack sit farther from the active hand. The face and the hand on the lid are the main focal points. The frame should suggest the instant before a small personal decision, not a product testimonial or a smiling payoff.

Render as clean gamey semi-realistic 3D achievable in a mid-range mobile engine: moderate polygon detail, simplified skin and fabric materials, broad baked/simple soft illumination from camera-left, gentle ambient fill, soft small highlights, stable natural exposure, simple contact shadows, sharp readable subject and background without lens blur. Warm-gray wall, one low-detail shelf with two abstract book shapes. Secondary props must be less detailed than the character. No words, logos, app UI, gameplay, numbers, notification graphics or watermark. No celebrity likeness, mascot, extra person, floating objects, extra fingers, warped laptop hinge, photoreal skin pores, cinematic grading, depth-of-field blur, lens flare, volumetric rays, film grain or heavy motion blur.
