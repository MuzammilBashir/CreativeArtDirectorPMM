# Performance Learning Log

Use this log to record evidence-backed learnings without duplicating Ad Bible principles or confusing correlation with causation.

## Learning record

~~~yaml
learning_id:
date:
game_id:
genre:
subgenre:
format:
platform:
audience:
country_or_market:
asset_ids:
creative_territory:
hypothesis:
variation_axis:
control:
metrics:
  impressions:
  spend:
  hook_rate:
  click_through_rate:
  install_rate:
  cost_per_install:
  retention_d1:
  retention_d7:
result:
observed_pattern:
possible_explanations:
confounds:
decision:
confidence: low | medium | high
promotion_status: observation | pattern | validated_learning
next_test:
~~~

## Promotion rules

- Observation: directly recorded result with limited evidence.
- Pattern: repeated or strongly supported observation within a defined scope.
- Validated learning: repeated evidence across comparable tests with known confounds considered.

Do not promote a learning into the Ad Bible unless it is cross-context and materially changes a general decision.

## Example

~~~yaml
learning_id: PUZZLE-001
game_id: example-game
subgenre: arrow-puzzle
creative_territory: challenge-and-mastery
hypothesis: Visible blocked moves will increase viewer intent to solve the puzzle.
variation_axis: first-three-second hook
result: pending
confidence: low
promotion_status: observation
next_test: Compare blocked-move hook against a clean-satisfaction hook.
~~~
