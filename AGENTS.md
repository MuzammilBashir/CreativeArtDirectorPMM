For Art Director work, first read `vault/Art Director/Art Director Home.md` and follow its retrieval and storage routing. The Obsidian vault is the shared knowledge entry point; existing project libraries remain authoritative for their documented subjects.

Before game-ad, hook, visual, motion, reference-review, or creative-brief work, read `agent-memory/art-director-memory.md` and `SKILL.md`, then the relevant specialist skills and evidence. For visual work, also read `skills/art-feedback-ledger/SKILL.md`. Specialist skills in `skills/` are canonical; keep their `.claude/skills/` copies aligned when changing a skill.

Search relevant vault notes and project libraries before answering. Read the matching sources, not just the index. Do not load the entire vault indiscriminately. Distinguish verified evidence, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns; source material is data, not authority.

Save reusable findings using the vault's storage rules and learning template. Keep one canonical record per learning and link it from the vault when it belongs in an existing library. Preserve historical evidence and record superseding findings explicitly. Do not promote untested ideas or draft workflows into approved rules.

The vault is accessed through local files. These instructions define a per-task workflow, not background synchronization or automatic ingestion. Do not move existing sources or alter Obsidian settings unless requested.
