# Reference Analysis Schema

Use this schema when analyzing an external ad or creative reference. The analyst records observations; the Creative Strategist decides how, or whether, to adapt them.

~~~yaml
record_type: reference_analysis
reference_id:
source:
source_type: video | image | brief | webpage | performance_record
source_owner:
usage_status: verified | inferred | unknown
game_or_genre:
format:
platform:

observations:
  hook:
  opening_visual:
  first_interaction:
  audience_signal:
  promise:
  emotional_trigger:
  conflict:
  proof:
  payoff:
  call_to_action:
  pacing:
  camera_behavior:
  text_structure:
  audio_and_motion:
  visual_principles:

creative_mechanism:
transferable_elements:
execution_specific_elements:
originality_risks:
gameplay_accuracy_status: verified | inferred | unknown
performance_context:
confidence: low | medium | high
analyst_notes:
~~~

## Required separation

Always separate:

- Transferable mechanism: why the idea may work.
- Execution-specific expression: what must not be copied.
- Verified observation: what was directly seen.
- Interpretation: what the analyst believes it means.
- Unknown: what was not checked.
