# Board study 02 — density revision

Requested target: approximately 85% filled / 15% empty on the existing 8×8 board.

Result: 54 occupied cells and 10 empty cells, or 84.375% / 15.625%. This is the nearest whole-cell approximation. Eleven blocks have eleven distinct matching-color gates; 216 studs implement four studs per occupied cell.

Geometry checks pass: integer footprints, uniform cell pitch, no overlaps, bounds, gate widths equal to perpendicular block spans. Every cell-step in a full clearing sequence was checked for collisions and correct gate passage. Conditions: straight sliding, no rotation, matching-color exits only. These remain test conditions, not source-game verification.

Verified exit order: blue left, indigo down, green right, yellow down, cyan up, violet up, teal up, white up, coral left, orange right, purple down. A valid sequence is established; shortest or unique solution is not claimed.

CAD assessment:

- Density target passes and yellow reads as more enclosed.
- The demonstrated straight-down route now needs three preparatory exits, rather than the original brief's two. This is an unresolved storyboard deviation, not an approved brief change.
- Higher occupancy does not itself prove greater difficulty. Many perimeter pieces still exit directly, and density quickly decreases during play.
- The top band repeats large rectangles; shape distribution remains visually mechanical rather than the reference's varied interlocking layout.
- White draws attention away from yellow, while coral and orange depart from the original cool-surroundings direction. Palette refinement is still needed.
- Uniform studs and exact gate geometry are retained. Flat bodies and outlined studs remain draft geometry, not approved glossy plastic materials.

Status: density and structural checks pass; final visual, challenge design and ad pacing approval withheld.
