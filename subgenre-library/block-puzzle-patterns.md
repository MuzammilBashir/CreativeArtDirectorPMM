# Block Puzzle Ad Patterns

## Scope

Patterns observed in Block Buster, Block Out, and Color Block Jam analyses. Apply when the game uses sliding, sorting, matching, or clearing physical-looking blocks.

## Observed patterns

### Immediate tactile action

Begin with a block sliding, fitting, exiting, or disintegrating. The mechanic itself is often the hook.

### Material identity

Toy-like, Lego-like, plastic, checkerboard, or chunky materials make the interaction feel physical and memorable.

### Progressive order

Start with a crowded board, clear smaller pieces, open a path, then release a larger or more important piece.

### Visible satisfaction

Use smooth movement, crunch or slide sounds, particle clearance, and a visibly cleaner board.

### Low-pressure positioning

No-timer or stress-free messaging can support the creative when verified and when the execution remains calm.

## Useful variation axes

- Toy, plastic, paper, stone, or abstract materials
- Calm satisfaction versus spatial challenge
- Single-piece focus versus multi-piece chain reaction
- Close interaction versus full-board strategy
- Real gameplay versus hybrid visual framing
- Sound-forward versus music-forward execution

## Common risks

- Material styling hides the actual mechanic.
- The board is too dense to read on mobile.
- The ad promises no pressure but uses frantic editing.
- A satisfying clear is staged or unavailable in the real game.
- Many variants differ only cosmetically.
