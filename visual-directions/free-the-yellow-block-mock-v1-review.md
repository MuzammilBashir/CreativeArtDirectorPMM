## Approved strengths

- Clear yellow target and matching two-cell exit gate
- Obstacle is emphasized without hiding the target
- Varied block shapes support the flexible puzzle concept
- Board frame and background match the supplied visual references
- Finger cursor clearly communicates interaction
- Yellow route indicators make the intended solution readable
- The mock supports the challenge-to-satisfaction emotional arc
- Hook and payoff are represented in a single concept board

## Production notes

- Treat the image as a key visual reference, not as a literal final frame.
- Separate the hook, failed attempt, reveal, payoff, and CTA into actual timed shots.
- Remove the extra external yellow block from the gameplay sequence unless it is intentionally the payoff transition.
- Rebuild the board using verified game geometry and movement rules.
- Preserve the target, blocker, gate, color hierarchy, and material language.
- Validate all text and safe areas in the final 9:16 export.
- Keep the red blocker highlight restrained and temporary.
