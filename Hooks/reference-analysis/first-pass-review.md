## Art Director handoff principle

Hook Analyzer identifies the evidence and the transferable idea. Art Director develops the story and visual directions; Higgsfield receives the resulting production prompts. Reference analysis must not force a particular story treatment or falsely establish Game DNA. New 4–5 second stories require intentional compression when their references are longer.
