# Creative Brief Template — V1

Use this brief before developing a mobile UGC or gameplay ad. Keep facts separate from assumptions and hypotheses.

## Brief metadata

```yaml
brief_id:
game_id:
game_title:
version:
created_date:
updated_date:
status: draft | concept-approved | in-production | review | approved | archived
owner: Autonomous Art Director
```

## 2. Game truth


Label important entries as `verified`, `inferred`, or `unknown`. Do not fill gaps with plausible but unverified game information.

Keep one main variable per hypothesis. Record competing explanations when the evidence is weak.

The hook should connect to the game’s actual value rather than relying on unrelated bait.

## 8. Art direction and ad-look system

```text
Visual direction status: existing | refine | create

```

When the visual direction is open, create two or three style tiles and select one before full production. References communicate principles such as pacing, framing, color, or energy; do not reproduce another creator’s distinctive execution.

## 9. Production plan

```text
ChatGPT tasks:

Required source assets:
New assets to generate:
Gameplay footage required:
Technical dependencies:
Expected iterations:
Allowed paid runs:

```

Never invent pricing. Mark estimates clearly and stay within the approved run limit.

## 10. Variant plan

```text
Control:
Variant A:
Variant B:
Primary variation axis:
What remains constant:
What result would change the next decision:
```

Create variants that test meaningful creative choices. Do not create many near-identical versions without a learning purpose.

## 11. Provenance and asset register

```yaml
asset_id:
file_path:
asset_type:
creator_or_tool:
prompt_or_source:
reference_sources:
license_or_usage_status: verified | inferred | unknown
created_date:
status: source | draft | approved | rejected | archived
notes:
```

Record all required source and generated assets. Unknown provenance must remain `unknown`; do not call it safe to use.

## 12. Risk and validation checklist

```text
Gameplay accuracy risks:
Visual consistency risks:
Platform risks:
Generation risks:
Copyright, trademark, likeness, or reference risks:
Technical risks:
Missing evidence:
External approval or escalation required:
```

Before production, confirm:

- Game, objective, audience, format, and placement are defined.
- The hypothesis and primary variation axis are explicit.
- Claims are supported or marked unknown.
- The hook, proof, payoff, and CTA are understandable.
- The required source assets are available.
- The visual direction and reference boundaries are clear.
- Provenance requirements are understood.
- The budget and paid-run policy are within the validated limits.

## 13. Approval handoff

```text
Selected concept:
Selected visual direction:
Production status:
Review framework: ad-review-framework.md
Expected approval status:
Review owner: Autonomous Art Director
```

After production, attach the completed review report. Do not mark the brief complete without a review status, provenance record, and explicit evidence of what was inspected.
